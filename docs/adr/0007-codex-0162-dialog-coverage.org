* ADR-0007: Cover the Codex 0.162 idle tail and folder-trust dialog
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: adr-0007-cover-the-codex-0162-idle-tail-and-folder-trust-dialog
:END:
** Decision
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: decision
:END:
Dated 2026-10-12. Within the existing codex_cli design (dialog words count
unless the screen ends with the real idle composer tail), the ~unless_tail~
footer row also accepts the two codex-cli 0.162.0 footers, a plain
~? for shortcuts~ and ~? for shortcuts~ followed by a singular or plural
~⚠ N warning(s) · f2 to view~. The dialog state also matches the 0.162.0
folder-trust modal by its whole lines ~Trust this folder?~ and
~enter continue · esc quit~. Dialog precedence, the dialog word rules and the
footer prefix/suffix controls are unchanged.

** Consequences
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: consequences
:END:
An idle 0.162.0 pane whose transcript or status line holds a dialog word (for
example a git branch named ~agy-ai-pair-yolo~ shown in the status line, or
prose saying "bypass") classifies :idle instead of holding every send. The
0.162.0 folder-trust modal classifies :dialog; before this change its
~› 1. Trust and continue~ option satisfied the idle prompt rule, so a queued
send and its Enter would have accepted the trust prompt. A busy screen that
shows a dialog word still classifies :dialog, as before; sends are held while
busy either way. A later Codex release that changes its footer or modal
wording needs a new capture and rule.

** Verification
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: verification
:END:
Real codex-cli 0.162.0 captures of the folder-trust and command-approval
modals (operator paths replaced by same-length placeholders) and an idle
screen reconstructed from the observed incident are fingerprint fixtures; the
approval-dialog delivery test covers both modals for plain and receipted sends.
The new tests fail on the previous rules and pass on these, and the transcript
controls of design r6 still hold. Rules are checked against captured text,
not against a live pane.
